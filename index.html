Toy Garden

A small garden of toys and childhood memories...

This toy reminds me of childhood afternoons.

I used to push this car across the floor.

This frog feels like rainy day memories.

A soft koala I used to hug when sleeping.

This rhino reminds me of zoo trips.

The seal makes me think of the ocean.

My first plush toy was a little rabbit given to me by my aunt.

This little duck reminds me of bath time as a child, floating quietly in warm water while everything felt safe and slow.

These blocks remind me of building little worlds, stacking shapes into something that only made sense to me.

I remember sitting on the floor, making it walk in circles. It didn't go anywhere, but I felt like I was in control of something.

I used to carry it everywhere, even when I didn't want to play. It just stayed in my hand, quiet and warm.

My dad bought it for me at a mall.
I don't remember the day clearly,
but I remember how tightly I held it after.

I liked knowing there was a right place for every piece.
It felt like everything could eventually make sense.

I left it on at night,
when the room felt too quiet.

I kept playing the same song,
even when I already knew how it would end.

I entered a yoyo contest in kindergarten.
I kept practicing the same move,
hoping I wouldn't mess it up.

I kept spinning it on the floor,
watching how long it could stay steady.

I made a wish without saying it out loud,
not knowing if it would go anywhere.

I kept hitting it,
even when it got too loud.

I kept pressing the buttons,
just to hear the sounds.

I rode it in the garden,
moving back and forth on the same path.

I bought it at a space museum during a school trip in elementary school.
I kept it for a long time after that.

I moved back and forth,
without going anywhere.

I kept stacking them,
until everything felt right.

I pulled it behind me,
just to hear it follow.
